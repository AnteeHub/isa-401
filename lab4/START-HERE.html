Lab 4 / One group task

Make the pelican ride your way.

Three people add three controls to one ready-made scene. Use your own AI agent. This practice is ungraded and requires no submission.

Try the reference   Open your project

1. Agree on your role

2. Ask your agent

Open this entire folder in your agent and paste the prompt. Choose your role. The agent can read the six steps in English (README.md) or Chinese (README_ZH.md).

Read README.md and AGENTS.md. I am member [A/B/C]. Follow the steps for my role. Build only my assigned control. Keep the scene and teammates’ files unchanged. Test what you can and report what you actually tested.

No project installation is needed. Save changes and refresh index.html. It is normal for teammates’ controls to remain unfinished in your own copy.

3. Combine and play

File handoff: send your one feature file and say what you tested. The integrator backs up a separate copy and asks their agent to follow docs/INTEGRATE.md. GitHub/Gitee groups can ask their agent to follow docs/PLATFORMS.md instead.

On the combined index.html: set speed to 2, choose Duck, then choose Close. All choices should work together. Set speed to 0 to stop. Reset scene should also reset the controls.

Your design

Use the reference to understand the required behavior. Do not copy its feature code or reproduce its control styling unchanged. Agree on your own control design and ask your agent to implement it from the requirements. Reuse the supplied scene and shared API.
