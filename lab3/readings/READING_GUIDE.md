# Lab 3 readings

**Ungraded. No submission required.** One main reading and two optional readings. You do not need to read all three during the lab.

Our project inspects a handwritten-digit classifier. These papers inform the progressive interface: show useful partial results and let people inspect them before completion. They are not papers that reveal every internal reason behind a neural-network prediction.

## 1. Main reading: Steering the Craft: UI Elements and Visualizations for Supporting Progressive Visual Analytics

Sriram Karthik Badam; Niklas Elmqvist; Jean-Daniel Fekete (2017). Computer Graphics Forum 36(3), 491–502. https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.13205

**Open:** Steering-the-Craft.pdf

**Read:** Figure 1 and Section 4.5 (UI Elements for Feedback and Control). Section 5 is optional.

**Discuss:** What can a person inspect or control before the computation finishes?

The original InsightsFeed system explores Twitter text. Our MNIST project borrows linked views, intermediate feedback and controls; it does not reproduce that system or its user study.

**Source and version:** https://www.cs.au.dk/~elm/pdf/insightsfeed.pdf — Author-hosted version.

## 2. Optional background: Progressive Analytics: A Computation Paradigm for Exploratory Data Analysis

Jean-Daniel Fekete; Romain Primet (2016). arXiv:1607.05162. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.05162

**Open:** Progressive-Analytics-2016.pdf

**Read:** Abstract and Section 1 (Introduction). Skip implementation details for this lab.

**Discuss:** Why might an early, incomplete result be useful during exploration?

**Source and version:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.05162 — arXiv preprint.

## 3. Optional design example: Studying Early Decision Making with Progressive Bar Charts

Ameya Patil; Gaëlle Richer; Christopher Jermaine; Dominik Moritz; Jean-Daniel Fekete (2023). IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics 29(1), 407–417; presented at VIS 2022. https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2022.3209426

**Open:** Progressive-Bar-Charts.pdf

**Read:** Figure 1 and Section 3.2 (Why Near-History Visualizations). Section 6 is optional.

**Discuss:** How could seeing recent changes affect a decision made before the final result?

The paper compares progressive bar-chart designs, including displays of recent history. A stable partial chart is not a guarantee that the final result will be the same.

**Source and version:** https://ieeevis.b-cdn.net/vis_2022/pdfs/v-full-1254.pdf — Conference-hosted author/prepublication PDF; placeholder publication fields retained.
