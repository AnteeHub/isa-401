Lab 2: How to prototype interactions?

  • TASK1: In the lab2 folder, open TASK1/TASK1-materials.pdf for the sketching activity.
  • TASK2: Read TASK2/optional-starter/README.md for the digital starter or TASK2/optional-reference/README.md for examples. For the paper activity, use the photo in TASK1.
  • TASK3: Read TASK3/video and data-api/README.md for the starter, or TASK3/reference/README.md for the completed example.

To run TASK2 or TASK3 digital materials, open the lab2 folder and start Start_Mac.command on macOS, Start_Windows.cmd on Windows, or run python3 serve.py. In the browser, open the task folder under the printed local address; keep the terminal running. Read the selected folder's README.md and API.md before working.
